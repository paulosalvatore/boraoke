=== WHY SONG+ARTIST MISSES (300-row sample, 49 misses) ===
Most misses are ORDER: "<a> - <b>" where the parser cannot tell which side is
the artist. Channels disagree (Karaokê Club writes artist first, Karaokê Show
Oficial song first) and many channels appear once. Per-channel templates fix
it where a channel's OTHER uploads show its convention; the rest needs an
artist lexicon (the TICKET-145 song spine) — the parser has no knowledge of
who is an artist. Second class: no separator at all ("Zé Ramalho Chão de Giz
Karaokê"), unsplittable without a lexicon. The rest are one-offs (medley
leftovers, version words like "em Forrozinho", non-Latin duplicates).
